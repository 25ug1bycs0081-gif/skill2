About us

Trusted hospital care with a human touch.

CityCare Hospital is a multi-specialty healthcare center focused on providing high-quality treatment, patient comfort, and clinical excellence in a warm and supportive environment.

Our multidisciplinary teams work together to provide personalized care across cardiology, orthopaedics, maternity, pediatrics, surgery, diagnostics, and rehabilitation.

Our Mission To deliver compassionate, technology-driven healthcare that improves lives.
Our Vision To become the region’s most trusted center for advanced, preventive, and patient-centered care.
